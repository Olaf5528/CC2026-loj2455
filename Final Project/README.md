  <h3>1. Assignment I'm Revisiting</h3>

    projection mapping assignment.
  </p>

  <p>

    mapping, especially through colors, movement, and different
    visual aesthetics. It's something I initially approached

    myself creatively and explore new ways of designing
    visual experiences.
  </p>

  <h3>2. How I Plan to Expand on My Previous Work</h3>

  <p>
    In my original assignment, I focused on designing
    different visual elements and experimenting with
    their movements. This time, I want to take that idea
    further by creating an environment where characters
    or different elements can move and interact.
  </p>

  <p>
    I'm not necessarily trying to create an entire world,
    but I would like to introduce some level of storytelling
    through projection mapping. I want the visuals to feel
    connected and create an experience rather than simply
    displaying moving shapes.
  </p>

  <h3>3. What I Need to Learn and Potential Challenges</h3>

  <p>
    One of my biggest challenges will be exploring projection
    mapping in more depth and understanding how to use it
    more creatively. I also want to experiment with incorporating
    visual elements that I design myself, rather than necessarily
    creating everything through code.
  </p>

  <p>
    Another aspect I would love to explore is adding audio
    to the projection mapping. Coming from a sound design
    background, I feel that sound could help bring the
    environment to life, strengthen the storytelling,
    and make the overall experience more immersive.
  </p>

  <p>
    I know there will be technical and creative challenges,
    especially when combining different visual elements,

    mapping and push my original idea further.
