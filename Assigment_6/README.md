-Main Idea

My main idea was to create an interactive clock that represents time through nature. I wanted the background to change depending on the time of day, just like the outside world. For example, at night, the background would be pitch black, while during the day, it would have brighter colors.

The background represents the hours, the sun represents the minutes, and the moon represents the seconds. I also wanted to add ocean waves that would move constantly without following any specific time. To make the scene feel more alive, I planned to include clouds and small birds appearing occasionally. An extra detail I wanted to add was stars appearing during the night.

What Happened During the Process

One of my biggest challenges was making the background change colors smoothly throughout the day. I experimented with different colors, but the best result I achieved was a brown background at night and a nice orange during the day.

Originally, I also wanted the sun to have small rectangular rays around it, like a typical drawing of the sun, but I found it more complicated than expected.

Fortunately, the sun and moon worked as I intended, moving according to the minutes and seconds. I also decided to add more layers of ocean waves because they gave the scene more depth and movement. Finally, I added a few clouds to make the composition feel less empty and more natural.

Although I couldn't achieve everything from my original idea, I was able to create a clock that uses natural elements to represent the passing of time while keeping the ocean constantly moving.
