  <h1>Final Project Proposal</h1>
  <h2>Exploring Sound Generation with p5.sound</h2>

  <h3>Assignment I'm Revisiting</h3>
  <p>
    For my final project, I want to revisit my work with
    the p5.sound library. Working with audio was one of
    the most challenging parts of the course, but also
    one of the most interesting to me.
  </p>

  <h3>Project Idea</h3>
  <p>
    I want to create an interactive tool for sound design.
    I'm currently exploring two possible directions.
  </p>

  <h4>Idea 1: Audio Generator</h4>
  <p>
    A tool that allows users to generate tones, experiment
    with different frequencies, and download their sounds.
  </p>

  <h4>Idea 2: Gesture-Controlled Sound</h4>
  <p>
    Combine p5.sound with HandPose to control sound through
    hand movements. Rather than creating a musical instrument,
    I want to explore making a creative sound design tool.
  </p>

  <h3>Challenges</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Understanding frequency and waveform generation.</li>
    <li>Controlling sound with sliders or hand gestures.</li>
    <li>Learning how to record and download audio.</li>
    <li>Combining HandPose with p5.sound.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Next Steps</h3>
  <p>
    I plan to start by experimenting with generating sounds
    using p5.sound. After that, I want to explore different
    ways of controlling those sounds and decide which
    direction to develop further.
  </p>
